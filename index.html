    For show owners: add Cashu to your feed

    nutstream reads the <podcast:txt purpose="cashu"> record proposed in Nodesignal's Cashu streaming spec: a NUT-18 payment request with no amount, not single-use, unit sat, and one HTTP POST transport. Enter your endpoint to get the line for your feed.

    How it works

    1. Find the show's endpoint. Route B: the feed's purpose="cashu" record, or, for a Sovereigncast show, its purpose="sovereigncast" host plus /api/community/ecash-payment (inferred; marked in the player). Route A: the feed's purpose="nostr" npub and its kind:10019 (relays, mints, P2PK key).
    2. Meter what you hear. Played seconds × your rate, in whole millisats. Seeking, muting and the pause switch stop the meter. The budget includes mint fees.
    3. Send in batches. Every few minutes (and when you pause or close the tab). Route B posts one NUT-18 payload to …?action=stream over HTTPS: no public record of what you listen to. Route A locks the proofs to the show's key and publishes a kind:9321 nutzap, signed with a fresh key each time; streaming nutzaps stay silent and under 100 sats so they count as streaming, not boosts.
    4. Nothing gets lost. Each step is saved before the next one runs; a lost reply is replayed and comes back as already redeemed. Retries follow the spec's reply table.
